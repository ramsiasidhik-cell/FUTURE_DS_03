Key recommendations
01 · Channel
Prioritise cellular outreach
Cellular converts at 14.9% vs only 4.1% for unknown contacts. Shift campaign budget toward cellular to maximise ROI.
02 · Timing
Focus on March and Q4
March (51%), September, October and December (44–47%) far outperform summer months. Reduce May–August spend.
03 · Segments
Target students and retirees
Students convert at 29% and retirees at 23% — both nearly double the 11.7% average. Blue-collar workers convert at only 8%.
